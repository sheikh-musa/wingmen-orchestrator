Sure — two ways, depending on whether the donation is already in:

1. New donation: record it (Donations → cash/PayNow/cheque), put in the donor with their email and tick "Tax-deductible (IRAS receipt)". On save it generates the receipt and emails it to that donor automatically — nothing else to do.

2. A receipt already made: go to the Receipts page, find it, and click the "Email" button next to it. It turns to a green "Sent" once it's gone.

Either way the donor needs an email on their record — if there isn't one, add it to the donor first and the Email button will appear.
