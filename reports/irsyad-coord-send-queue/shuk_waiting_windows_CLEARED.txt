A quick note on why some things sit for a day, since I know you've felt it.

When we change anything that touches money, permissions, or your donors' data, we hold it for a day before it goes live — even after our own reviewers have signed it off. That's not us being slow to look at it. The work is finished and checked before the clock even starts.

What the day actually buys is the person we didn't ask. It's already paid for itself once: someone outside the review noticed that a safeguard we had all just approved was only half-applied. Reviewers agreeing with each other doesn't catch that. An open window does.

What changed today, in your favour: that wait now only applies to money, permissions and data. Everything else goes the moment the reviewers clear it — no clock at all. That's most of what we build for you.

And you do get a say in it. If something matters more to you than what we're doing, tell me and it goes first. If you want something sooner, I'll tell you straight whether the wait is the reason and what a smaller version would cost you. The one thing I won't do is shorten the wait on a money change — that protection is the point of it, and it's yours more than it's ours.

None of this changes anything you've already been told.
