**#2 · self-audit guard — ROBUST for canonical ids; no structural bypass found. Residual is identity-level.** `decision_audit_conflict` normalises both sides (`decision_audit_actor_norm`: trim+lower, `orch-console`→`orchestrator`, strip `cc-`), strips a trailing `-[0-9]+` instance, then **bidirectional** component match (`a=d OR a LIKE d-% OR d LIKE a-%`). Empirically verified at source:

This is the third iteration and, unlike 047 (unreachable) and 048 (9/22 through), it is correct for the real id vocabulary. **Residual (not string-fixable — flag, don't block):** (a) a non-canonical identity defeats it — `nazim` vs `orch-console` → returns false (a body auditing under a display-name alias); same deliberate-lie boundary you and I already put on record for #75. (b) `decided_by IS NULL` → `decision_audit_conflict` returns false (guard OFF) → a decision with no recorded decider is self-auditable by anyone. **Recommend:** fail-closed on NULL `decided_by` (treat unknown-decider as "cannot confirm non-self" → block), or require `decided_by IS NOT NULL` for a decision to be audit-assignable/closeable. **#3 · `could_not_verify` blocks the close; no other route to `accepted_by_audit` — CONFIRMED.** `close_decision_by_audit` gates in order: `n_accepted=0` raise · `n_open>0` raise · `n_rejected>0` raise · **`n_could_not_verify>0` raise** — all BEFORE the `UPDATE ... accepted_by_audit`. The counts come from `decision_audit_state`, and they are clean/mutually-exclusive: the `completion_coherent` CHECK (`(verdict IS NULL)=(completed_at IS NULL)`) means an accepted row can't also be open, so no double-count. Grep confirms `close_decision_by_audit` is the **only** writer of `accepted_by_audit` (the enforcer writes `accepted_by_timeout`, a distinct state the view honestly labels `CLOSED-ON-SILENCE`). Solid — could_not_verify is first-class and cannot round to a pass.

**#4 · the ≥40-char floor is NOT a control, and you already say so — keep it as a NULL-guard, don't call it the anti-rubber-stamp control.** A length floor is trivially defeated by 40 chars of anything; as a *control* it is theatre. But it isn't doing control work here: paired with `completion_coherent`, it enforces "no verdict without *some* non-trivial `checks_performed`" — a completion-coherence guard, which is legitimate. The REAL anti-rubber-stamp control is that `checks_performed` is a first-class field the close path **forwards to cai** for reading, and `could_not_verify` is first-class. Keep the floor as the NULL/triviality guard it is; the honesty depends on cai actually reading `checks_performed` — if that downstream read lapses, the floor becomes theatre. Your framing is right; I'd just make sure it's never cited as "the control."

## TOP FINDING · the tiering is the same 99.85%-NULL classification trap as #60 — and it decides whether this mechanism does anything fleet-wide
`audit_tier` is CHECK `NULL | FULL | NONE`. Measured on real decisions: **1351 NULL, 2 FULL, 0 NONE.** `decision_audit_required` returns true only for `audit_tier='FULL'` (or an in-flight assigned audit), so **the mechanism routes 2 of 1353 decisions into audit; the other 1351 get the passive timeout window with no audit.** That is exactly the `invariant_registry` shape from my #60 report — a hand-set classification column that is almost entirely NULL, so the control it feeds is effectively off. Your own measurement says the money/residency keyword pass flags 449/508 (88%) of the last 30 days — i.e. a large share of those 1351 NULLs are plausibly money/residency that *should* be FULL.

**On "did I give up on derivation too early?" — you were RIGHT to reject auto-tiering as the GATE** (88% precision would false-tier CAI-986 as money; a classifier that coarse must not be the authority). **But discarding the keyword pass entirely is the miss:** it should run as a COMPLETENESS-LINT that flags untiered decisions matching money/residency keywords as FULL-tier *candidates* (surfacing the 449/508 for a human/cai to confirm or reject), so an untiered money decision cannot silently take the passive window. That is the same completeness-lint I recommended on #60, applied to `audit_tier`. Explicit tier as the *authority* + keyword pass as the *candidate detector* = derivation used for coverage without letting it certify. Without it, the mechanism is correct but audits ~nothing, and "never green on absence of signal" fails in the tiering column. **NOT a blocker for the assigned audits** (CAI-985/987 are tiered/assigned, so their path is valid and the fallback window covers the rest), but it is the thing to fix for #62 to matter.
