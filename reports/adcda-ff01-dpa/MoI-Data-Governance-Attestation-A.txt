                MINISTRY OF INTERIOR - DATA-GOVERNANCE ATTESTATION
              Cross-Border Hosting of ADCDA Trainee Personal Data


PURPOSE

This attestation confirms one specific point that a data-processing agreement
and ordinary consent cannot: that this specific class of data is permitted,
under the controlling UAE / Abu Dhabi government-data rules, to be stored and
processed outside the UAE (in Singapore). It is to be completed by a
representative of the Ministry of Interior (MoI) / Abu Dhabi Civil Defence
Academy (ADCDA) who can speak to the Ministry's own data-classification and
governance rules. Wingmen (the processor) relies on it as the Ministry's
authoritative statement of its own regulatory position.


PARTIES AND DATA

Controller (government body): ____________________________________________
    [Ministry of Interior - UAE / Abu Dhabi Civil Defence Academy (ADCDA),
     a body under the MoI - confirm exact legal entity name]

Processor: ____________________________________________
    [Wingmen legal entity], operator of the cosem training-examination platform.

Data in scope ("the Data"): the personal data of ADCDA trainees processed on
the cosem platform - specifically staff / military-linked identification
numbers, rank, and names (Arabic and English), together with the associated
training-examination records.

Hosting location: Singapore - Amazon Web Services region ap-southeast-1 -
which is OUTSIDE the territory of the United Arab Emirates. The Data is
single-tenant isolated, encrypted at rest and in transit, org-scoped access,
service-role-only writes, with an append-only audit log; full terms are in the
accompanying Data Processing Agreement.


ATTESTATION

The undersigned, as a representative of the Ministry of Interior, having
considered the Ministry's own data-classification, information-security and
data-governance rules as they apply to the Data described above, attests that:

    Under the controlling UAE / Abu Dhabi government-data rules, the Data is
    NOT subject to any requirement that it reside within the United Arab
    Emirates, and it is PERMITTED to be stored and processed in Singapore
    (AWS ap-southeast-1) as described above.

The undersigned further confirms:

  1. The Data's classification under the Ministry's own rules has been
     considered specifically for this cross-border arrangement. This is not a
     general assurance.

  2. The undersigned is authorized to make this specific data-classification /
     localization determination on behalf of the Ministry of Interior, and
     this determination reflects consultation with, or originates from, the
     Ministry's competent data-governance / information-security authority. It
     is not the undersigned's own personal assessment. (General authority to
     sign documents for the Ministry is not sufficient for this attestation.)

  3. The undersigned understands that Wingmen relies on this attestation and
     will not write any real trainee data to the Singapore store unless the
     statement above is truthfully attested.

If the undersigned cannot truthfully make the statement above, this form must
not be signed - in which case the data must reside in the UAE and the parties
will use a UAE-hosted arrangement instead.
